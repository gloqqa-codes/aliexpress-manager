Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AliExpress Manager is a browser extension that helps you save, organize and download media from AliExpress product pages.

What data the extension handles

  • Items you save: product title, thumbnail link, price and page link, stored only when you click "Save to bucket".
  • Your settings: the position of the floating buttons.

Where it is stored

All of this is stored on your own device, in your browser's local extension storage. It is not sent to the developer or to any server, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.

What the extension does not do

  • No accounts, analytics, tracking or advertising profiles.
  • No collection of your browsing history, purchases or personal information.
  • No remote code.

Network activity

  • "Check links" requests the pages of products you saved, directly from AliExpress, to see whether they still exist.
  • Files you choose to download are fetched by your browser from AliExpress and saved where you pick.

Affiliate links

When you open an AliExpress product page, the extension adds the developer's affiliate tracking tag to the link. This does not change the price you pay and no personal data is attached to it. Links that already contain affiliate tags are left unchanged. AliExpress may record the visit under its own privacy policy.

Your control

Delete saved items from the extension at any time, or remove the extension to erase all stored data.

Questions

If you have a question about this policy, open an issue on this page's GitHub repository (the Issues tab at the top of the repo).

AliExpress Manager is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by AliExpress.
